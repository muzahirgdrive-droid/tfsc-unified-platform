THE TWO APPLICATIONS
TFSCSalesCRM is the sales workspace. ScribeAlignContractStudio is the complete
current TFSC Compliance application, including Personal Injury Collections,
company letterheads and Word/PDF generation. They have separate websites,
databases and access grants. Optional OIDC can share the identity provider.
The agreements application retains its own account and company authorization.

PILOT LIMITS — DO NOT PRESENT AS 50,000-USER CERTIFICATION
This build is ready for controlled functional review, not an unqualified
production launch for 50,000 users. PostgreSQL/Docker configuration is included,
but this machine has not run the hosted stack or a production load test.
Reporting currently aggregates source rows and builds a scoped roster; daily
materialized rollups, a reporting worker and a measured capacity baseline remain.
There is no configured holiday/leave calendar or historical employment roster.
Scores reflect current lead qualification, while dated target revisions preserve
past target settings. Qualification events are retained in the audit trail.
The fixed 10/20/30/50/100 scoring ladder is not an editable scoring-rule engine.

The sheet workspace is paste-and-review, not a complete Excel replacement with
virtualized editable cells. XLSX preview returns the first 1,000 rows of one
visible populated data sheet. Larger workbooks must be split; the original
89,914-row workbook has not been automatically imported into production.
No background resumable import worker or automatic per-sheet user mapping yet.

Lead qualification assigns the selected/original owner; weighted round-robin
allocation and shared-switchboard identity adjudication remain to implement.
The strict unique normalized-phone ownership rule may require supervisor review
for practices sharing a switchboard. Supervisor account creation is not enabled;
managers and above create accounts. Custom permission-role authoring is pending.

Google requires your OAuth app and organizer account. Calendar synchronization
is explicit, not a background watch subscription. Vonage uses tel: through the
registered desktop handler; product-specific integration/call verification is
pending. Email sending and electronic-signature providers are not configured.
OIDC requires an identity provider; configure MFA there. No email invitation or
self-service password recovery service is included. Managers can reset scoped
user passwords, with forced change at next login.

CRM contract snapshots are refreshed explicitly and are account-specific.
The source of truth and authorization for contract bytes remain in TFSC.
There is no durable bidirectional webhook/event delivery service yet.
Schema additions use create_all; a formal migration/rollback system, database
row-level policies and independently protected audit storage remain deployment
hardening work. Audit records are append-only through app routes, not immune to
a database administrator. Browser access cannot prevent photographs or all data
copying. Do not promise impossible theft prevention.
