How to sign

  1. Select Start (or Next) to go to each field marked for you.
  2. Select a Sign or Initial field. The first time, choose how your signature looks: a style made from your name, drawn with your finger or mouse, or an uploaded image.

  4. Select Finish. Everyone receives the completed documents by email when all parties have finished.
