    Help

    Signing takes a few minutes. Your entries are kept if you need to stop.

    1. Select Start, then follow the tab on the left. It takes you to each field in order: the initials on every page, then your signature, then your title.
    2. Select an Initial or Sign field. The first time, choose how your signature and initials look: a style, drawn with your finger or mouse, or an uploaded image.
    3. Fill in the other fields. Your name and today's date are filled in for you, and your title is copied into the other documents.
    4. Select Finish. Everyone receives the completed documents and the Certificate of Completion by email.
