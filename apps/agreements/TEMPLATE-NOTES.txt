TFSC Compliance: retained contract sources

The BAA uses the supplied October 6 sample, with variable company, client and date
fields. Its supplied Georgia governing-law wording is retained.

Services, responsibilities and fees are Sections 1, 2 and 3. The supplied billing,
credentialing, scribe and virtual assistant wording is retained. Other service
scopes retain the existing application's wording. Personal Injury Collection is
available with FTE or percentage of eligible net collections pricing.
WordPress and custom website projects have separate prices.

The original company letterhead files and supplied signature images are retained.
Word crops remove blank signature margins without changing the source image.
The images are placed on signing lines. Visible signatures can still be copied
or photographed; this application does not create cryptographic signatures.

This release changes application access, storage, history and rendering. It does
not certify legal compliance or replace approval of the reusable contract wording.
