QUICK GUIDE

From client details to contract.

  1. Enter the parties and effective date.

    Open an existing client to reuse their details. Choose “Start a new agreement” before opening a client to build a standalone service engagement.

  2. Choose your documents and services.

    Generate the BAA, the Services Agreement, the Mutual NDA, or any combination. Every selected service adds its applicable scope, responsibilities and price.

  3. Review the terms and save location.

    The initial date is today. Change it whenever needed. The BAA may use a separate date. The source letterhead is preserved.

  4. Preview, then generate.

    Word and PDF copies are saved in the client’s folder. Use Save draft to reopen client details and pricing later. Generation also saves your draft.

Local files, under your control
