Quick guide

  1. Create the agreement.

    Create agreement › choose the company, the client and the documents (BAA, Services Agreement, Mutual NDA), then Generate. Word and PDF copies are saved to the client.

  2. Send it for signature.

    Select Send for signature on the generated agreement, or Start › Send an envelope. Signature, initials, name and date fields are placed for you; drag any of them to adjust.

  3. Follow it to completion.

    Home shows what needs your action, what is waiting for others and what is completed. Everyone receives the completed documents and the Certificate of Completion by email.

  4. Find anything later.

    Clients keep every agreement, envelope, signed copy and file. Reports export to Excel; Admins see the full activity log.
