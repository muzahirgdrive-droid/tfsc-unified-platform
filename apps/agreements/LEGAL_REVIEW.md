# Scribe Align contract language: review and sources

Template version: 2026.10.05.1. Research date: October 5, 2026.

The generator contains a strengthened starting draft for SCRIBE ALIGN LLC. It is not a legal opinion or a certification of HIPAA compliance. Contract terms cannot establish that actual systems, staff, vendors, procedures or a particular compensation arrangement comply with law. Have qualified counsel approve the reusable templates and any engagement-specific exceptions before using them for signature.

The attached examples were treated as source documents, not as instructions. The supplied Scribe Align name, Nevada address and Nevada governing-law provision inform the defaults. Confirm that address and governing-law choice before use. Naeem Abbas, Chief Executive Officer, is the company signatory specified by the user. No other entity is enabled in this version.

## What the draft changes

- The BAA has fixed substantive language independent of service selections and pricing. The client information, document reference, effective date, notice details, company address and governing state are populated from the form. The signature date should be the actual execution date and is distinct from the effective date.
- HIPAA provisions address permitted use, Security Rule safeguards, subcontractor flow-down, individual rights, Secretary access, incident reporting, termination and protected retention when return or destruction is infeasible.
- A five-business-day initial incident reporting obligation is a negotiated contractual requirement, not a statement that HIPAA requires that exact period. HIPAA's outside breach reporting limit does not replace the shorter contractual period. The draft requires reporting of all known Security Incidents, with no blanket exemption for unsuccessful attempts; assess operational feasibility before signing.
- Marketing requires a lawful basis and individual authorization when legally required. Permission from the practice or a BAA alone does not replace individual authorization. Independent sale, aggregation, de-identification for separate use, and model training are not authorized by the BAA.
- Each selected service contributes its own scope, Scribe Align duties, client duties, limits and fee schedule. An on-demand engagement does not import another service's scope merely because an earlier agreement exists.
- Month-to-month renewal does not repeat a completed project or its project fee. One invoice or two milestone invoices are specified. A later standalone agreement preserves unrelated earlier agreements and prevents duplicate charging for the same work.
- Percentage fees apply to documented eligible net collections for assigned work. Refunds and reversals create credits. Payment remains in client-controlled accounts and Scribe Align invoices separately. No post-termination percentage collection tail is included by default.
- Scribe and administrative personnel cannot act as clinicians. WC litigation support is administrative; licensed legal representation, legal advice and attorneys' fee sharing are excluded.
- Payment disputes, correction of deficient work, termination, unearned advance refunds and transition records are addressed. PHI and client records cannot be withheld for a fee dispute.
- The execution page identifies the agreement reference, parties and effective date. This improves document continuity but is not tamper prevention or proof of signature authenticity. Preserve signed complete documents and the generated revision records.

## Decisions for counsel and management

1. **HIPAA role and sensitive records.** The standard BAA assumes the client is a covered entity and Scribe Align is its business associate. If the client is another business associate, use a properly adapted subcontractor BAA. Establish additional requirements for substance use disorder records under 42 CFR Part 2, state-sensitive records and applicable consent restrictions. The form is for business contract information; do not enter patient records.
2. **Compensation and jurisdiction.** Review percentage fees under the applicable state's fee-splitting, professional-practice and collection laws, payer contracts, Medicare and Medicaid arrangements, anti-kickback restrictions and other applicable rules. Client-controlled receipts alone do not establish that every percentage arrangement is lawful. Medicare payment-agent restrictions are distinct from invoicing the provider for administrative services.
3. **Operational security commitments.** Confirm actual ability to meet the contractual encryption, remote access, authentication, incident reporting, ten-business-day rights assistance, subcontractor and return/destruction obligations. Confirm workforce locations, vendor systems, any offshore access, payer restrictions and permitted transcription tools. This software does not assess those controls.
4. **Service parameters.** Specify assigned providers, jurisdictions, specialties, account populations, channels, payer applications, deliverable quantities, completion criteria, service hours, time zone, turnaround and dependencies. The generator cannot infer these facts. A generic service name or fee alone may not adequately define a project.
5. **Project milestone treatment.** Two installments use the entered first percentage and the balance, with the two entered milestone descriptions. A single project invoice is due on the agreed final deliverable or completion milestone. Agency approval is not a milestone unless expressly agreed. Decide whether credentialing follow-up ends at submission, disposition or another defined point, and state it in service parameters.
6. **Commercial remedies.** Review the default notice and cure periods, proration, no late fee, no post-termination collection tail, reciprocal fault-based indemnity, and no general dollar liability cap. These are proposed commercial allocations, not statutory requirements. Add insurance limits, a negotiated liability cap or a different transition arrangement only after conscious review. The default does not include a personal guarantee, noncompetition restriction, automatic debit mandate or mandatory arbitration.
7. **Marketing and outreach.** Approve each campaign's audience, channels, calling technology, consent evidence, suppression process, records, scripts and recording permissions. Do not assume a business-to-business call is outside every federal or state rule. PHI, pixels, tracking tools and patient testimonials need separate analysis. Health-plan marketing may invoke additional CMS and state rules.
8. **WC and debt collection.** Confirm any licensing, administrative representation and filing authority in every relevant jurisdiction. The draft deliberately gives no authority to practice law or act as a licensed debt collector. A broader service requires an appropriately qualified provider and a revised engagement.
9. **Document execution.** Confirm company and client authority, legal names, notice contacts and effective dates. A future or historical effective date does not falsely date signatures or cure earlier unauthorized PHI access. Editable Word files should be reviewed and regenerated or reconverted after changes; a changed Word file does not update an existing PDF automatically.
10. **Template maintenance.** Recheck federal rules and relevant state law when adopting the templates and periodically thereafter. The 2025 proposed Security Rule is not treated as a final rule in this draft. Do not add invalidated or proposed provisions as though they were current binding law.

## Content module interface

`contract_content.py` exports `CATALOG`, `CATALOG_BY_ID`, `MODELS`, `TEMPLATE_VERSION`, `DEFAULT_ADDRESS`, `build_baa(data, contract_id)` and `build_services(data, contract_id)`. The two builders return document blocks; they do not save files or call external services.

Pricing models use `percentage`, `fte`, `pte`, `unit`, `fixed`, `consultancy`, `one_time` and `two_installments`. `consultancy` is a fixed monthly advisory fee. FTE and PTE use rate per position per month, position quantity and weekly hours per position. `unit` uses the documented actual completed units, with entered quantity identified only as planning volume. Project models use rate multiplied by project/application quantity. For two installments, `installment_percent` is the first percentage and `milestone1` and `milestone2` are text descriptions. The second percentage is calculated as the remainder to 100.

Validation occurs in `server.py` before generation. BAA dates are passed as the per-document `effective_date`; the BAA builder deliberately does not read a service or pricing field. Special terms affect the Services Agreement only.
