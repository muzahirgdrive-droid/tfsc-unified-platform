CURRENT BRIDGE, STEP BY STEP
1. Supervisor confirms a qualified lead as a successful sale in CRM.
2. Super Admin explicitly links the CRM account to an existing TFSC user UUID.
   TFSC Super Admin separately maps that CRM actor UUID to the TFSC account.
3. CRM POST /api/leads/{id}/agreements checks role, scope, signed_up state and
   entitlement. It builds a client draft with one of the three legal entities.
4. Server-to-server POST /api/integrations/v1/crm/handoffs is HMAC authenticated.
   TFSC checks account activity, its company grant, idempotency and replay nonce.
5. TFSC saves an editable draft and returns a single-use token valid for 10 minutes.
   The mapped user signs into TFSC and redeems it at POST /api/handoffs/redeem.
6. The owner completes services and pricing in TFSC, saves the draft and submits
   POST /api/reviews/submit. An independent, authorized administrator approves
   the exact content and template/signature hash through /reviews/{id}/decision.
7. The approved revision enters the existing document queue. The worker checks
   approval and permissions before rendering and before publication; creates
   Word/PDF; stores identical bytes in the TFSC database and output folders.
8. CRM explicitly synchronizes POST /api/clients/{id}/agreements/sync. TFSC's
   signed /api/integrations/v1/crm/status supplies scoped review/file metadata.
   CRM stores an actor-specific snapshot; files are downloaded from TFSC at
   /files/{id} after TFSC authorization. CRM does not duplicate contract blobs.

SIGNING CONTRACT
Secret: CRM_TFSC_SECRET == TFSC_CRM_SHARED_SECRET, at least 32 random characters.
Headers: X-TFSC-Timestamp (Unix seconds), X-TFSC-Nonce (new URL-safe nonce),
X-TFSC-Signature (hex HMAC-SHA256). Canonical message is five newline-separated
strings: timestamp, nonce, uppercase HTTP method, request path, SHA256(raw body).
No query string is part of the current signature. Timestamp tolerance is 300 sec.
Content-Type application/json; signature covers exact bytes, not reserialized JSON.
The receiver consumes the nonce within its transaction. Use a new nonce on retry
and the same business idempotency key/body. Same-key handoff retries issue a fresh
redemption token. Never place session cookies or credentials in cross-site URLs.
CRM_TFSC_URL is the public browser URL. CRM_TFSC_INTERNAL_URL optionally specifies
container transport; the request Host still matches the public TFSC origin.

CURRENT BOUNDARIES / GAPS
This is an implemented pilot, not the entire requested enterprise architecture.
No durable bidirectional delivery/inbox/outbox worker; synchronization is manual.
No external email delivery or electronic signature platform is configured.
Issued means released/generated, not signed by a client or legally executed.
Do not bypass review to make an integration appear successful. Do not infer a
signature image is a cryptographic signature. Do not claim data cannot be copied.
Review integration and authorization under concurrent revocation, mapping changes,
retries and expired tokens before production. Keep contract bytes authoritative
in TFSC and do not silently merge clients by name or phone.
